## What Is a Native IP and What Can It Be Used For?

Let’s focus on the term **“native IP.”**

Simply put, a native IP is an IP address that genuinely belongs to the local data center or region. It is not an IP broadcast from another location.

Many streaming platforms such as **Netflix, HBO, Hulu**, and similar services check whether your IP address really belongs to the country or region you are trying to access. If you are using a broadcast IP or a typical data center IP, the platform may block access directly.

This LisaHost plan uses a **native U.S. IP**. Based on actual testing, it can unlock services such as:

- Netflix U.S.
- Amazon Prime Video
- HBO

If you usually want to watch U.S.-exclusive shows or play U.S. servers of online games, such as **World of Warcraft U.S. servers** or **Path of Exile**, this IP can be useful.

However, it is important to note that streaming platforms frequently update their IP detection policies. Just because an IP works today does not mean it will work forever. Still, at least for now, the native IP performance of this data center appears to be relatively stable.
